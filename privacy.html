Who we are

Vail Breeze Properties (“Vail Breeze,” “we,” “us”) offers furnished stays and property-management services in Denver and Englewood, Colorado. We are responsible for personal information described in this notice. Contact: vailbreezeproperties@gmail.com · (720) 828-2021.

At a glance

  • You can browse without an account.
  • Stay and owner planners run in your browser. Creating a summary does not send it to us. Booking requests are sent to us only when you press “Send booking request.”
  • We do not add analytics, advertising pixels, or social-media embeds to this website.
  • Bookings and payments take place on Airbnb. This website does not collect payment-card details.

Information and how it is used

Website delivery. The hosting and security providers process technical information such as your IP address, requested pages, browser information, and request time to deliver and protect the site. Cloudflare may set a necessary anti-bot cookie. See our Cookie Notice.

Local planning. Dates, guest counts, property preferences, goals, and optional contact details remain in the current page while you prepare a summary. Website code does not save these entries to a database, cookies, or browser storage. Reloading or closing the page clears the page’s copy; your browser may independently restore fields or offer autofill. Clipboard copies and downloaded files remain under your control.

Booking requests. When you send a booking request from a property page, the details you enter (dates, guests, name, email, optional phone and message) are delivered to our email through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-forwarding service. We use them to reply about availability, price, and payment, and to manage any resulting reservation. FormSubmit processes the submission under its own privacy terms (opens a new tab). Do not include payment-card details in a request; payment is arranged separately and securely.

Contacting us. If you choose to email or call, we receive the information you share and use it to answer questions, discuss a stay or property-management services, and keep relevant correspondence. “Open email draft” hands your summary to your chosen email application. You must review and send it there. Your email or telephone provider also processes that communication. Please do not send payment-card details, identity documents, passwords, or other sensitive information through the planner or ordinary email.

Service providers and outside websites

Hosting and security services support website delivery. Our published contact email uses Gmail. Airbnb independently handles information you provide on its platform, including bookings, payment, and host messages, under Airbnb’s Privacy Policy (opens a new tab). No planner entries are automatically shared with Airbnb. Fonts, photos, and video are served from this site; embedded maps, external video players, and tracking widgets are not used.

We do not sell personal information collected through this website or use it for targeted advertising or automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or security.

Retention and security

Planner entries are not retained by the website. You control saved summaries. Correspondence may be kept for responding to your request, an ongoing business relationship, and applicable recordkeeping or legal needs; it is not subject to a promised automatic deletion period. Hosting and communication providers apply their own retention practices. Contact us about a specific record.

The published website uses HTTPS. We minimize the information the website collects, but no method of transmission or storage is risk-free. Read our Security Information. Providers may process information in the United States and other locations where they operate.

Your choices and requests

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, or to opt out of certain processing. Email vailbreezeproperties@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request,” or call (720) 828-2021. Describe the request and the information involved; do not include identity documents in your first message. We may need proportionate verification before disclosing or changing information. If a request is declined, you can reply with “Privacy appeal” to request a review and may contact your relevant regulator.

This website does not sell data or use targeted advertising, regardless of your browser’s Global Privacy Control (GPC) setting. There is no optional tracking to turn off. . Browser controls can clear or block cookies; blocking security cookies may affect access.

Colorado and other U.S. state privacy rights

Where the Colorado Privacy Act or a similar state law applies, you may confirm whether we process your personal data and request access, correction, deletion, or a portable copy, and opt out of targeted advertising, sale of personal data, and profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects. We do not engage in any of those opt-out activities. We respond to verified requests within 45 days, which may be extended once by 45 days where reasonably necessary, and we will tell you why. Requests are free of charge up to twice a year. You may use an authorized agent; we may ask the agent for proof of authority.

Appeals. If we decline your request, reply with the subject “Privacy appeal.” We will respond within 45 days. If you are not satisfied, you may contact the Colorado Attorney General (opens a new tab) or your state’s regulator.

Visitors from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland. You may also object to processing based on legitimate interests, request restriction, and lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. Information may be processed in the United States.

Do Not Track and GPC. Because we do not track visitors across sites, a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal does not change how this website behaves; we treat a GPC signal as a valid opt-out request where the law requires it.

Reviews and testimonials

Guest and owner reviews shown on this website are published with the reviewer’s permission, including any name, title, and photograph. A reviewer may ask us to remove their review at any time by contacting vailbreezeproperties@gmail.com.

Children and changes

This website is intended for adults arranging stays or property services, not children under 13. Please contact vailbreezeproperties@gmail.com if you believe a child has sent personal information. We may update this notice as the website or its information practices change; the date above identifies the current notice.
