How do I book a stay?

Choose a home, pick your dates and group size, and send a booking request from its page. We reply by email to confirm availability, your total price, and secure payment. Your stay is booked once you accept and pay. Nothing is charged when you send a request.

Where can I see current prices?

Prices vary by season, dates, weekends, holidays, and last-minute availability. We confirm the nightly rate, cleaning fee, and taxes for your exact dates in our reply to your booking request. We do not display fixed nightly prices here.

Are all the homes in Denver?

Four listings are in Denver. Mountain View is in Englewood, Colorado. Each home’s page shows its neighborhood. The exact address is shared once your stay is confirmed.

Can I arrange an extended stay?

Denver Skyland is listed for extended stays, and the other listings may offer dates that suit a longer visit. Every home allows stays of 28 nights or more. Mention your dates and plans in your booking request and we will tailor a quote.

How do I contact Vail Breeze?

Call (720) 828-2021 or email vailbreezeproperties@gmail.com. The optional planner creates a summary on your device. You can copy, download, or open an email draft; review and send it in your email app. Booked through Airbnb already? Message us there or call.

What are the management rates?

The proposed rates are 20% for short-term Airbnb/Vrbo rentals, 15% for furnished executive rentals, and 10% for long-term unfurnished rentals. The fee basis, services, and any additional charges need written confirmation.
