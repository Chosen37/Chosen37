Visitors outside the United States

Under the EU and UK ePrivacy rules, cookies that are strictly necessary to provide a service you request, including security cookies, do not require consent. Because this website uses only strictly necessary cookies, no consent banner is shown. If we ever add analytics, advertising, or other optional cookies, they will stay off until you opt in, and you will be able to withdraw consent as easily as you gave it.
