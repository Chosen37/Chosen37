About this home

Historic character, a chef’s kitchen, and room to gather in Curtis Park.

Nestled in Denver's coveted Curtis Park Historic District, this Beautiful home offers a captivating fusion of historic charm and contemporary living. Step inside and discover a light-filled open floor plan, accentuated by soaring ceilings on all three levels. The gourmet kitchen boasts sleek modern appliances, including a gas cooktop, pot filler, and convenient in-island microwave. Relaxing on the inviting front porch, and fenced corner lot. in this spacious and serene space.

The space

Denver’s Curtis park Gem

Welcome to your perfect Denver home base – a beautifully updated 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom house tucked into the charming, tree-lined streets of historic Curtis Park, the heart of downtown Denver’s most walkable and vibrant neighborhood.

Step inside and feel instantly at home: soaring ceilings, hardwood floors, an open-concept living space flooded with natural light, and a chef’s kitchen that makes morning coffee or late-night tacos equally enjoyable. Three spacious bedrooms mean everyone gets privacy and comfort – after a Broncos, Avalanche, Nuggets, Rockies game or a concert.

Location doesn’t get better than this. You’re just blocks from RiNo’s buzzing arts district, Larimer Square’s rooftop bars, and some of Denver’s top-rated restaurants (think Beckon, Uchi, Dio Mio, and endless craft breweries). On game day, it’s an easy walk or quick rideshare to:

Spend your mornings sipping coffee on the front porch watching the neighborhood wake up, your afternoons exploring the city parks and galleries, and your evenings strolling to dinner and nightlife – then come home to a quiet, residential street where you can actually relax and get a great night’s sleep.

Whether you’re in town for a Broncos weekend, a concert series, a family getaway, or just to experience everything Denver has to offer, this house is the ideal launchpad – stylish, comfortable, and perfectly located so you can live like a local while having downtown Denver at your doorstep.

Book now – this Curtis Park gem gets snapped up fast, especially on game weekends and during festival season!
