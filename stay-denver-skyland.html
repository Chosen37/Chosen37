About this home

A furnished Skyland stay with a dedicated workspace and a fenced yard.

The space

Welcome to your new home away from home! This peaceful and centrally located fully furnished 2-bedroom, 1-bath apartment offers a comfortable and convenient living experience. The second bedroom is perfect for working at home with a full desk, ergonomic chair, and a Murphy bed to give you space to spread out. Enjoy a fully equipped kitchen, in-Home laundry, and a cozy living area. Move-in ready, just bring your suitcase. All utilities included, making budgeting a breeze. Ideal for both short-term and long-term stays of 1 to 6 months. High-speed Wi-Fi and a smart TV for entertainment. Dedicated parking spot for your convenience. Pets welcome with an additional deposit. This is a very pet friendly home with a dog door at the back door and a fully fenced front and back yard. Close to Downtown, City Park, and the Zoo.
